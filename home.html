How it works

Three steps, then it just waits.

Stick it on once. After that, it works whether your phone is in your pocket or across the city.

01

Stick it on the glass

Peel and place the Scan To Move tag on your windscreen, bike, or helmet. It sits there, quietly.

02

They scan, they type

Anyone opens their phone camera, scans, and sends you a message. No app, no sign-up, nothing to install.

03

You get pinged — privately

You see it instantly and can reply in the app. Neither of you ever sees the other's phone number.

The person who scans learns nothing about you.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Does the person who scans see my phone number?+

No. They never see your number, name or registration plate. They send a message through the app, and you reply the same way — they never see your number either.

Do they need to install anything?+

No. They scan the sticker with their phone camera and a web page opens. They type a message there. Only you, the owner, use the app.

What if I lose the sticker?+

Mark the tag lost or delete it in the app and it stops working immediately. Until you do, whoever holds the sticker can still message you.

Is my data safe?+

Your details are locked to your account in the database and are never sent to a person who scans your tag. See our privacy policy for exactly what we store.
