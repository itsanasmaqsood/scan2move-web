Questions

The things people ask first

Does the person who scans see my phone number?+

No. They never see your number, name or registration plate. They send a message through the web page, and you reply in the app. They never see your number either.

Do they need to install anything?+

No. They scan the sticker with their phone camera and a web page opens. They type a message there. Only you, the owner, use the app.

What if I lose the sticker or my vehicle?+

Turn on Lost mode in the app and whoever finds it is asked to tell you where it is. You can also pause or delete the tag and it stops working immediately.

Is my data safe?+

Your details are locked to your account in the database and are never sent to a person who scans your tag. See our privacy policy for exactly what we store.
