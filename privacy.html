Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Scan To Move puts a QR code on your vehicle so a stranger can reach you when your car is blocking someone, your lights are on, or your window is open — without either of you learning the other's phone number.

This policy describes exactly what the app stores. It was written against the actual database schema, not from a template. Where we do not collect something, we say so.

Contact for any privacy question or request: anas.official2017@gmail.com

2. What the other person can see

This is the part that matters most, so it is stated plainly.

A person who scans your tag can see: the label you gave the vehicle (for example "Blue Hatchback") and its type. That is all.

They cannot see: your name, your email address, your phone number, your registration plate, your account, your other vehicles, or any of your other conversations. This is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app: the lookup a scanner performs returns three fields and no others, and every table is closed to them.

You cannot see: who scanned your tag. There is no name, number or email attached to them.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers or insurers.
  • We do not run advertising in the app.
  • We do not build a profile of you for marketing.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from More → Settings → Delete account inside the app.

This is a real deletion, not a deactivation. It removes your profile, your vehicles, your tags, every scan and message attached to them, your notifications, and your login itself. Your QR codes stop resolving immediately. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email anas.official2017@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • See the data we hold about you
  • Correct anything wrong
  • Delete your account and data (or do it yourself, above)
  • Export your data in a machine-readable form Email anas.official2017@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, these rights may be granted to you by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), the GDPR (European Economic Area and UK), or the CCPA/CPRA (California). We apply them to everyone regardless of where you are.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: contract (running the account and delivering messages you or a scanner send), and legitimate interests (preventing abuse through rate limiting, and fixing crashes).

8. Children

Scan To Move is for people who own or drive a vehicle and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

8a. Our website

  • Scan page. When you scan a tag, your browser is given an anonymous session (no name, email or number). It is kept in your browser's local storage so you can come back and read the owner's reply. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Sticker orders. The order form on our home page does not send anything to us by itself. It prepares an email that you choose to send. We use what you put in it (name, email, delivery address and the vehicle label) only to make and deliver your sticker, and delete it once the order is complete, unless the law requires us to keep invoice records.
  • Fonts. Our home page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google and Google can see your IP address. See Google's privacy policy. The scan page and legal pages make no third-party requests.
  • We set no cookies and run no analytics on the website.

9. Security

  • Every table is closed by default; access is granted per-row to the owner only.
  • The lookup used when someone scans a tag runs server-side and returns nothing that identifies you.
  • QR tokens are 128-bit random values generated by the server, not by the app.
  • Rate limits are enforced in the database, so they cannot be skipped by a modified client.
  • IP addresses are salted and hashed before storage.
  • Traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS); data is encrypted at rest by Supabase.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, please email anas.official2017@gmail.com rather than disclosing it publicly, and we will work with you.

10. Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the "last updated" date, and — for a change that materially affects you — tell you in the app before it takes effect.
